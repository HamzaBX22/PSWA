# Partnership With Allah (PSWA) 🤲

> **মাসিক সদকা ও দান ট্র্যাকার (Monthly Sadaqah & Giving Tracker)**  
> *A minimalist, offline-first Islamic giving and Zakat tracker web application designed with care, barakah, and elegance.*

---

## 🌟 Features (বৈশিষ্ট্যসমূহ)

- **🌐 Bilingual Support:** Instant toggle between **বাংলা (Bengali)** and **English**.
- **👁️ Privacy / Stealth Mode:** Tap the eye icon to blur financial figures when using in public.
- **🎯 Monthly Giving Target:** Automatically calculates your monthly goal based on your salary and chosen giving percentage.
- **🏷️ 8 Donation Categories:** Color-coded tracking for Food, Medical Aid, Mosque & Madrasa, Relatives & Orphans, Education, General Sadaqah, Zakat, and Others.
- **📊 Analytics & Insights:** Visual breakdown of monthly expenditures and distribution.
- **🧮 Integrated Zakat Calculator (2.5%):** Instant Nisab guide and calculation based on gold, silver, cash, merchandise, and debts.
- **🎯 Special Campaign Goals:** Track custom funds like *Ramadan Food Drive*, *Winter Cloths*, or *Mosque Construction*.
- **📜 Daily Quran & Hadith Cards:** Beautifully presented authentic verses and Hadiths on charity in Arabic, Bengali, and English.
- **📄 Printable Monthly Report:** Generate and print/download a formatted monthly summary receipt slip.
- **💾 Export & Import Data:** Full backup and restore capability via JSON format.
- **📱 PWA & Offline Support:** Installable on mobile and desktop as a standalone app with offline caching.

---

## 🚀 How to Run Locally

Simply clone or download this repository and open `index.html` in any modern web browser:

```bash
git clone https://github.com/HamzaBX22/PSWA.git
cd PSWA
```
Then double click `index.html` or open it with your browser.

---

## 🔒 Privacy & Data Storage

All your financial logs and settings are stored locally on your device using `localStorage`. No data is ever sent to any external server.
